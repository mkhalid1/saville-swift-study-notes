# Sources

Facts about the tests come only from Saville Assessment's own pages and documents. Live pages were checked on 24 November 2026. If something has changed, please [open an issue](https://github.com/mkhalid1/saville-swift-study-notes/issues).

## Important: archived Saville documents

Saville redesigned its website in 2026. Its technical summaries, which give the question counts and time limits, are no longer at their old addresses, and the live candidate pages say timings are shown inside each candidate's own platform. We therefore cite Saville's own documents through the Internet Archive, with the snapshot date. They are Saville documents, unchanged, but they are not on Saville's live site. **Your own instructions screen is the final word on timings for your test.**
