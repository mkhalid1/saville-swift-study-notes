# 15-minute diagnostic

There are two Swift tests in this repo, so there are two short diagnostics. Do the one for your test. If you do not know which you have, read [which Swift test you have](which-swift-test.md) first, or do both.

Each track has nine items, three for each sub-test. The items are original, written for this repo, and easier than the hardest items you will meet. Their job is to show which sub-test to study first. Set a 7-minute timer per track, which is a little slower than the real pace.

## Track 1: Swift Analysis Aptitude

### Verbal Analysis

> From January, the firm will repay the train fare for any client visit whenever the ticket is booked through the travel portal at least three days ahead. Taxi fares will be repaid only for journeys that start after 10pm. The policy applies to staff in all of the firm's UK offices.

Choose True, False or Cannot say.

**A1.** A member of staff in one of the firm's UK offices who books a train ticket for a client visit on the portal a week ahead will have the fare repaid.

**A2.** A taxi journey that starts at 8pm will be repaid.

**A3.** Most staff travel to client visits by train.

### Numerical Analysis

Monthly unit sales of two products.

| Product | January | February | March |
|---|---|---|---|
| X | 400 | 460 | 529 |
| Y | 500 | 450 | 405 |

**A4.** By what percentage did sales of X rise from January to March, to one decimal place?

A. 15.0% &nbsp; B. 28.0% &nbsp; C. 30.0% &nbsp; D. 32.3% &nbsp; E. 129.0%

**A5.** In which month were combined sales of X and Y highest?

A. January &nbsp; B. February &nbsp; C. March &nbsp; D. January and February equally &nbsp; E. All months equal

**A6.** By what percentage did sales of Y fall from January to March?

A. 10% &nbsp; B. 19% &nbsp; C. 20% &nbsp; D. 23.5% &nbsp; E. 81%

### Diagrammatic Analysis

Two operators. Work out what each does.

| Input | Operator | Output |
|---|---|---|
| ●○■ | E | ○●■ |
| ○■□ | E | ■○□ |
| ●○■ | F | ●○□ |
| ○○ | F | ○● |

**A7.** ■○● → E → ?

A. ○■● &nbsp; B. ●○■ &nbsp; C. ■●● &nbsp; D. □○● &nbsp; E. ■○○

**A8.** ●□○ → E → F → ?

A. □●○ &nbsp; B. □●● &nbsp; C. ●□● &nbsp; D. ■●● &nbsp; E. ○□●

**A9.** ? → F → ●●□. What was the input?

A. ●●□ &nbsp; B. ○○■ &nbsp; C. ●●■ &nbsp; D. ●○■ &nbsp; E. □●●

## Track 2: Swift Comprehension Aptitude

### Verbal Comprehension

> Parcels left in the post room must be collected within five working days. Uncollected parcels are returned to the sender. The post room is open from 8am to 4pm on weekdays.

Choose True, False or Cannot say.

**C1.** The post room opens at 8am on weekdays.

**C2.** Parcels can be collected up to two weeks after they arrive.

**C3.** Most parcels are collected on the day they arrive.

### Numerical Comprehension

| Item | Price | Quantity ordered |
|---|---|---|
| Pens | £3.20 per box of 10 | 15 boxes |
| Notepads | £1.80 each | 40 |

**C4.** What do the pens cost in total?

A. £32.00 &nbsp; B. £45.00 &nbsp; C. £48.00 &nbsp; D. £50.00 &nbsp; E. £480.00

**C5.** What is the total cost of the order?

A. £72.00 &nbsp; B. £104.00 &nbsp; C. £112.00 &nbsp; D. £120.00 &nbsp; E. £126.00

**C6.** How many individual pens are in the order?

A. 15 &nbsp; B. 25 &nbsp; C. 100 &nbsp; D. 150 &nbsp; E. 1,500

### Error Checking

Which row of the copy contains an error?

**C7.**

```
Original
Row 1  REF 7730-41 | Carver Ltd
Row 2  REF 7730-46 | Dunmore & Co
Row 3  REF 7731-02 | Ellison Group
Row 4  REF 7731-09 | Farrant Hire

Copy
Row 1  REF 7730-41 | Carver Ltd
Row 2  REF 7730-46 | Dunmore & Co
Row 3  REF 7731-20 | Ellison Group
Row 4  REF 7731-09 | Farrant Hire
```

A. Row 1 &nbsp; B. Row 2 &nbsp; C. Row 3 &nbsp; D. Row 4 &nbsp; E. No error

**C8.**

```
Original
Row 1  12/05 | 14:30 | Bay 3
Row 2  12/05 | 15:10 | Bay 8
Row 3  13/05 | 09:45 | Bay 1
Row 4  13/05 | 11:20 | Bay 6

Copy
Row 1  12/05 | 14:30 | Bay 3
Row 2  12/05 | 15:10 | Bay 8
Row 3  13/05 | 09:45 | Bay 1
Row 4  13/05 | 11:20 | Bay 6
```

A. Row 1 &nbsp; B. Row 2 &nbsp; C. Row 3 &nbsp; D. Row 4 &nbsp; E. No error

**C9.**

```
Original
Row 1  Ms R. Whitcombe | £412.80
Row 2  Mr T. Osei | £96.15
Row 3  Mrs L. Varga | £1,130.00
Row 4  Mr D. Kaur | £58.40

Copy
Row 1  Ms R. Whitcomb | £412.80
Row 2  Mr T. Osei | £96.15
Row 3  Mrs L. Varga | £1,130.00
Row 4  Mr D. Kaur | £58.40
```

A. Row 1 &nbsp; B. Row 2 &nbsp; C. Row 3 &nbsp; D. Row 4 &nbsp; E. No error

## Answers

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations</summary>

**A1: True.** The ticket was booked on the portal more than three days ahead, for a client visit, by staff in a UK office. All the conditions are met.

**A2: False.** Taxis are repaid only for journeys starting after 10pm.

**A3: Cannot say.** The passage says nothing about how staff usually travel.

**A4: D, 32.3%.** 529 − 400 = 129. 129 ÷ 400 = 32.25%, which rounds to 32.3%. *Why not E:* 129 is the rise in units, not the percentage.

**A5: C, March.** Combined sales: January 900, February 910, March 934.

**A6: B, 19%.** 500 − 405 = 95. 95 ÷ 500 = 19%. *Why not C:* two falls of 10% do not add to 20%, because the second fall is taken from a smaller number.

**A7: A, ○■●.** E swaps the first two shapes. The second example rules out "flip the colours of the first two", which would give ●□□.

**A8: B, □●●.** E swaps the first two: □●○. F flips the colour of the last shape: □●●.

**A9: C, ●●■.** F flips the last shape's colour, so undo it: ●●□ becomes ●●■.

**C1: True.** The post room is open from 8am on weekdays.

**C2: False.** Parcels must be collected within five working days, and uncollected ones are returned, so two weeks is too long.

**C3: Cannot say.** The passage gives no information about when parcels are usually collected.

**C4: C, £48.00.** 15 boxes × £3.20 = £48.00. *Why not E:* that prices each pen at £3.20.

**C5: D, £120.00.** Pens £48.00 plus notepads 40 × £1.80 = £72.00.

**C6: D, 150.** 15 boxes of 10 pens.

**C7: C, Row 3.** 7731-02 was copied as 7731-20.

**C8: E, no error.** All four rows match.

**C9: A, Row 1.** Whitcombe was copied as Whitcomb: the final e is missing.

</details>
