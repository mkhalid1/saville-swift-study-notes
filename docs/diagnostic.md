# 3-minute diagnostic

There are two Swift tests in this repo, so there are two short diagnostics. Do the one for your test. If you do not know which you have, read [which Swift test you have](which-swift-test.md) first, or do both.

Each track has three items, one for each sub-test. The items are original, written for this repo, and easier than the hardest items you will meet. Their job is to show which sub-test to study first. Set a 3-minute timer per track, which is a little slower than the real pace.

## Track 1: Swift Analysis Aptitude

### Verbal Analysis

> From January, the firm will repay the train fare for any client visit whenever the ticket is booked through the travel portal at least three days ahead. Taxi fares will be repaid only for journeys that start after 10pm. The policy applies to staff in all of the firm's UK offices.

Choose True, False or Cannot say.

**A1.** Most staff travel to client visits by train.

### Numerical Analysis

Monthly unit sales of two products.

| Product | January | February | March |
|---|---|---|---|
| X | 400 | 460 | 529 |
| Y | 500 | 450 | 405 |

**A2.** By what percentage did sales of X rise from January to March, to one decimal place?

A. 15.0% &nbsp; B. 28.0% &nbsp; C. 30.0% &nbsp; D. 32.3% &nbsp; E. 129.0%

### Diagrammatic Analysis

Two operators. Work out what each does.

| Input | Operator | Output |
|---|---|---|
| ●○■ | E | ○●■ |
| ○■□ | E | ■○□ |
| ●○■ | F | ●○□ |
| ○○ | F | ○● |

**A3.** ●□○ → E → F → ?

A. □●○ &nbsp; B. □●● &nbsp; C. ●□● &nbsp; D. ■●● &nbsp; E. ○□●

## Track 2: Swift Comprehension Aptitude

### Verbal Comprehension

> Parcels left in the post room must be collected within five working days. Uncollected parcels are returned to the sender. The post room is open from 8am to 4pm on weekdays.

Choose True, False or Cannot say.

**C1.** Parcels can be collected up to two weeks after they arrive.

### Numerical Comprehension

| Item | Price | Quantity ordered |
|---|---|---|
| Pens | £3.20 per box of 10 | 15 boxes |
| Notepads | £1.80 each | 40 |

**C2.** What is the total cost of the order?

A. £72.00 &nbsp; B. £104.00 &nbsp; C. £112.00 &nbsp; D. £120.00 &nbsp; E. £126.00

### Error Checking

Which row of the copy contains an error?

**C3.**

```
Original
Row 1  REF 7730-41 | Carver Ltd
Row 2  REF 7730-46 | Dunmore & Co
Row 3  REF 7731-02 | Ellison Group
Row 4  REF 7731-09 | Farrant Hire

Copy
Row 1  REF 7730-41 | Carver Ltd
Row 2  REF 7730-46 | Dunmore & Co
Row 3  REF 7731-20 | Ellison Group
Row 4  REF 7731-09 | Farrant Hire
```

A. Row 1 &nbsp; B. Row 2 &nbsp; C. Row 3 &nbsp; D. Row 4 &nbsp; E. No error

## Answers

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations</summary>

**A1: Cannot say.** The passage says nothing about how staff usually travel.

**A2: D, 32.3%.** 529 − 400 = 129. 129 ÷ 400 = 32.25%, which rounds to 32.3%. *Why not E:* 129 is the rise in units, not the percentage.

**A3: B, □●●.** E swaps the first two: □●○. F flips the colour of the last shape: □●●.

**C1: False.** Parcels must be collected within five working days, and uncollected ones are returned, so two weeks is too long.

**C2: D, £120.00.** Pens 15 × £3.20 = £48.00 plus notepads 40 × £1.80 = £72.00.

**C3: C, Row 3.** 7731-02 was copied as 7731-20.

</details>
