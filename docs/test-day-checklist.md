# Test-day checklist

Swift tests are short, so small problems on the day cost a lot. Most are easy to prevent.

## The day before

- [ ] Find your invitation email. Saville's candidate page says your personal link is the only way in, and there is no general login page.
- [ ] Log in to check the timings and practice questions in your platform. Saville says they are matched to the assessments you are taking.
- [ ] Use a laptop or desktop. Saville's practice page says Swift Analysis and Swift Comprehension cannot be taken on a mobile phone.
- [ ] Check your screen: Saville recommends a resolution of at least 1366 x 768 on a PC, the latest browser version, JavaScript turned on and a download speed of 3 Mbps or more.
- [ ] If you need an adjustment, contact the employer before you start. Saville's candidate page says to do this as early as possible.

## One hour before

- [ ] Quiet room, notifications off, other tabs closed.
- [ ] Pen and paper ready, plus a calculator for the numerical sub-tests. Saville's practice tests list a calculator as recommended there.
- [ ] Do one or two of Saville's practice questions to warm up.

## In the first minute

- [ ] Read the instructions for each sub-test. Each one starts with its own instructions and its own clock.
- [ ] Note where the timer is.
- [ ] Write your three reminders at the top of the paper: "point to the sentence", "base value first", "write the rule".

## During the test

- [ ] Check the clock every two questions.
- [ ] Hit your per-question limit, choose and move on.
- [ ] In Error Checking, compare in chunks and trust "no error" when rows match.
- [ ] Answer on your own. Saville's practice test notes that "advanced checks are built into the assessment to monitor your completion and detect the potential use of AI".
- [ ] Do not let a hard sub-test throw you. The total score counts all three.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Note which sub-test felt hardest. It helps if you are retested.
- [ ] For questions about results or next steps, contact the employer. Saville's candidate page says the organisation that invited you decides on feedback.
- [ ] If something went wrong technically, use Saville's support form with details of what you saw and which assessment you were taking.

## One last timed run

If you have a day spare, do one more timed run. For Swift Analysis, the [free Saville Swift practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/saville-swift?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=saville-swift) uses the same length and section order.
